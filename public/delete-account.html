Welcome to Sri Aditya Developers. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by following the instructions below.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to sriadityamobile1@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the following information in your email:
    • Full Name
    • Email address or username used for registration
    • Phone number (if applicable)
  3. Our support team will verify your request and process deletion within 7–10 business days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Login credentials and authentication tokens
  • Order, booking, or service history (unless legally required to retain)

Data That May Be Retained

  • Government-issued IDs or verification documents (retained for legal compliance if applicable)
  • Transaction or payment history for accounting or legal purposes (retained for up to 1 year)
